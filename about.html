About Rapport

Rapport Institute for Psychoeducation is a human and organisational development institution committed to advancing human well-being and promoting human-centred systems through education, training and practical initiatives.

Championing Rehumanisation

Guided by our philosophy of Championing Rehumanisation, we seek to put people at the centre of development and societal progress.

It means putting people at the centre of development and promoting systems that recognise human needs, dignity and potential. We believe systems are built with and by people; when people flourish, systems become more effective.

Mission

To champion the human dimension of development by transforming knowledge into practical initiatives that advance individual growth, institutional effectiveness and collective well-being.

Vision

A world where humanity remains at the centre of development, institutional practices and societal progress.

What we do and don’t do

What we do

  • Psychoeducation
  • Training
  • Workshops
  • Research
  • Advocacy
  • Community engagement

What we don’t do

  • Counselling
  • Psychotherapy
  • Clinical treatment

Where specialised services are needed, we may facilitate referrals to qualified professionals and relevant organisations.
