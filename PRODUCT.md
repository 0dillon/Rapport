# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Institutional leaders, school administrators, teachers, HR directors, organisational managers, and community organisers in Nigeria seeking psychoeducational training and capacity building for their teams and communities.

## Product Purpose

Rapport Institute for Psychoeducation is a human and organisational development institution that advances well-being and fosters human-centred systems through applied psychoeducation, workshops, and institutional training ("Championing Rehumanisation").

## Positioning

Non-clinical psychoeducation and organisational development. Rapport transforms psychological knowledge into practical, institutional capacity-building initiatives rather than individual clinical therapy or counseling.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate institutional credibility, programme topics, and collaborative methodology before submitting a tailored programme request that routes directly into WhatsApp (or email) with pre-filled details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML, single CSS architecture, vanilla JavaScript (no build step or external UI framework dependencies). The user explicitly instructed: "leave the typography and content as is, change the designs to have a more professional, minimalist appeal. While following proper design heirarchy, making sure it communicates effectively using its colors and font."

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: Rapport Institute for Psychoeducation (Rapport)
- Slogan: Championing Rehumanisation
- Brand Mark: Yellow head above speech-bubble ring with three dots (representing a person in conversation)
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans variable font
- Content: All existing copy, headings, and structure preserved as-is

## Evidence on Hand

- Authentic vector mark assets (`images/logo-mark.svg`, `images/logo-lockup.svg`)
- Confirmed contact channels (`rapportng@gmail.com`, WhatsApp `+2348147549034`)
- Real curriculum topics across Schools, Workplaces, and Communities
- Strict absence of synthetic testimonials, fabricated metric counters, or generic stock photography

## Product Principles

1. **Human-Centred Restraint:** Trust is earned through precision, balance, and intentional whitespace rather than loud decorative gimmicks.
2. **Professional Minimalism:** Every border, container, and color tint serves semantic hierarchy and effortless readability.
3. **Purposeful Chromatic Hierarchy:** Use Rapport Blue, Deep Navy, refined neutrals, and the brand yellow deliberately to draw focus to action and meaning.
4. **Accessible Craft:** Uncompromising legibility, WCAG AA contrast, clear keyboard focus, and smooth responsive behaviour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA compliant contrast ratios across all surfaces, accessible form validation with clear non-color error indicators, visible focus indicators, screen-reader text, and native `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
